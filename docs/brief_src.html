VEIL — Verifiable Entity Integrity Layer

Track 3 · Secret Wards · Hide the identity. Preserve the medicine. · Offline · CPU-only · Python stdlib · TITAN (known-good-9) · github.com/ThatKJ/VEIL

1. What VEIL is

VEIL is a clinical privacy release-assurance engine for fragmented, multilingual Indian hospital exports (CSV/TSV, super-header/EAV/stacked/box tables, nested/stringified/coded JSON, FHIR R4, HL7 v2, notes across Latin, Devanagari, Bengali, Gurmukhi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, and 8 Indic digit scripts). It resolves each patient across files, scripts, and hospitals using 50-bit keyed pseudonyms, measures risk on the pooled profile, escalates only rare classes, and audits every export with Assurance 2.0. Zero LLMs, zero ML, zero network.

3. Distinctive engineering & prior-art attribution

  • Pooled longitudinal privacy (adapted from ARX) — risk is assessed per resolved patient across records, bringing risk-driven equivalence-class anonymisation to pooled multilingual free text and structured schemas offline.
  • Zero-false-merge identity graph (adapted from Splink & Dedupe) — Fellegi–Sunter blocking + TF weighting with cluster-wide given-name conflict vetoes, one-edit national-ID typo tolerance, and safe household-phone/ration-card handling.
  • Format-general scrubbing & protection (UCSF Philter, Presidio, i2b2, CleverCSV, FHIR R4) — handles unquoted delimiters, coded schema maps, JSON-in-CSV, embedded JSON blocks, URL paths, and 8 Indic numeral systems while protecting doses, labs, doctors, and nurses.
  • Truth-free Release Assurance (tools/veil_passport.py, dev/demo) — pairs every physical file 1:1 with SHA-256 report entries and audits residue, clinical expression/interval preservation, and pseudonym placement without ground truth.

  • RAKSHA_SECRET never hits disk/logs; 50-bit PSN-XXXXXXXXXX deterministic (1.0) & collision-safe (0/1M); strict is_relative_to(sanitized) containment; hostile symlinks/binaries/>64 JSON quarantined.
  • 36/36 tests pass (unit, security, contracts, 200k pseudonym uniqueness, Assurance 2.0, Mutation Lab, UI). Official check_submission.py --run: READY TO SUBMIT. MIT license.
  • Local harness scores omit sealed weights and finite sweeps cannot prove formal anonymity outside the published attacker model; Passport checks remain heuristic where ground truth is absent.
  • Cross-script Latin/South-Indic merging still stays conservative on rare spelling gaps to maintain 0 false merges; H20 multi-row block probes stay unaligned under single-row CSV truth lookup. Docker inactive locally.
