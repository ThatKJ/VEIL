1. What VEIL is

VEIL is a clinical privacy release-assurance engine for fragmented, multilingual Indian hospital exports (CSV/TSV, super-header/EAV/stacked/box tables, nested/stringified/coded JSON, FHIR R4, HL7 v2, notes across Latin, 10 Indic scripts incl. Assamese/Odia/South-Indic, and 9 numeral systems). It resolves patients across files, scripts, and hospitals using 50-bit keyed pseudonyms, applies ARX minimal-loss pooled risk control, protects clinical probes/eponyms (`pyDeid`/Philter/medspaCy transfers), and verifies every export. Zero LLMs, zero ML bloat, zero network.

  • Zero-false-merge identity graph (vs Splink-TF & RapidFuzz) — benchmarks proved generic Splink-TF suffers FM=609–2,902 without whole-cluster vetoes; VEIL adds Assamese ৰ/ৱ, Odia, HL7 ^ & honorific/title stripping (`Mr`/`Sardar`/`Baideu`) at 0 FM everywhere.
  • Clinical Context & Classical Model Tournament (`pyDeid`, Philter, medspaCy, `python-crfsuite`, `ahocorasick_rs`) — E2E ablations across 5 datasets selected pyDeid eponym/disambiguator & `SN` nursing transfers while rejecting 52–71 KB CRFs (`+0.000` recall, `-0.002` probes on `dev`) and Rust `ahocorasick_rs` (`-2.8%` slower on 100k_unique CSVs) to keep runtime 100% pure stdlib.

6. Security & Honest Limitations

  • `RAKSHA_SECRET` never hits disk/logs; 50-bit `PSN-XXXXXXXXXX` deterministic (1.0) & collision-safe (0/1M); strict `is_relative_to(sanitized)` containment; symlinks/binaries/>64 JSON quarantined.
  • 37/37 tests pass (unit, security, contracts, 200k pseudonym uniqueness, Assurance 2.0, Mutation Lab, UI). Official `check_submission.py --run`: READY TO SUBMIT. MIT license.
  • Local harness scores omit sealed weights and finite sweeps cannot prove formal anonymity outside the published attacker model; Passport facility-name heuristics can flag allowed hospital names on ground-truth-free runs.
  • Unknown writing scripts abstain safely from cross-script merging to protect `0` false merges; `H20` multi-row block probes stay unaligned under single-row CSV truth lookup. Docker inactive locally.
