1. Problem & Core Product Story

Aggregated hospital exports interleave English, Devanagari Hindi, Hinglish, and encoded identifiers across CSV/TSV tables, nested JSON, FHIR R4 Bundles, HL7 v2 messages, clinical notes, filenames, and broken files. Conventional record-level redaction either leaks relatives/encoded payloads, corrupts clinical meaning (Dr. vs relatives, C/O fever, dosages), merges homonymous patients, or leaves pooled quasi-identifiers (age, district, diagnosis, stay length) open to registry re-identification.

VEIL's approach: Instead of merely masking rows, VEIL discovers PII across heterogeneous exports, resolves fragmented patient identities cross-script with contradiction vetoes, issues keyed HMAC-SHA256 pseudonyms, evaluates pooled patient-level exposure, applies adaptive privacy rules while preserving medicines and exact hospital stays, and independently verifies the release via a five-pillar Privacy Passport and adversarial mutation harness.

3. Five Technical Differentiators

  • Cross-Script Identity Firewall: Script-independent phonetic skeletons across Latin + 9 Indic blocks (\u0900–\u0D7F) & 9 numeral systems link fragmented records via strong IDs and bounded inverted blocking. Whole-cluster cannot-link constraints (gender, birth year gap, Aadhaar/ABHA/PAN conflict, kin/surname mismatch, household ration cards) prevent transitive merges and abstain conservatively (0 observed false merges on practice).
  • Adaptive Longitudinal Privacy: Pools each resolved patient's quasi-identifiers across files. Applies minimal-loss ARX k_dist (5-yr age band + district suppression at k<5) before escalating rare profiles, rare ICD-10 diagnoses (≤3 patients), or seniors (≥80) to consistent 10-yr bands, paired with per-patient keyed ±180d date shifts keeping exact lengths of stay (LOS) intact.
  • Cryptographic & Determinism Discipline: Domain-separated HMAC-SHA256 keyed strictly by env RAKSHA_SECRET (exit 2 if empty) with deterministic sorted collision resolution guarantees byte-identical outputs across CPU counts and OS platforms.
  • Independent 5-Pillar Privacy Passport: Audits any release pair with zero truth across Privacy (incl. Base64/Hex/URL/\uXXXX decoding, PSN-PAN context boundaries, clinician/institution filtering, and row/column-aligned unshifted DOB checks), Clinical, Temporal, Identity, and Structural (FHIR refs incl. #id & CSV row counts), distinguishing PASS/FAIL/WARNING/SKIPPED/INCONCLUSIVE.
  • Adversarial Mutation Lab & Lean Footprint: Pure Python 3.9+ stdlib runtime (0 models, 0 network) continuously verified against 16 controlled defect injections (identifiers, dosages, lab values, LOS shifts, broken FHIR refs, unshifted DOB cells).

5. Privacy–Utility Trade-Off Analysis

  • District Drop & Clinical Preservation: Dropping rare k<5 districts (arx_k5_dist_drop) sacrifices 92 geographic labels out of 4,855 preserved probes (retaining 98.1% clinical/medical items) to cut local profile re-identification from 43/48 (direct masking) to 10/48 (9/48 with secret grid).
  • Why Aggressive DOB Suppression Was Rejected: Suppressing birth dates reduces local re-identification further (3/48) but collapses temporal utility from 0.98 to 0.68 and lowers estimated total scores across all 3 tested benchmarks. VEIL preserves ICD-10 codes, dosages, vitals, and exact stay lengths (LOS) to keep research utility high.

6. Security & Encryption-at-Rest Design

Official CLI (main.py): External RAKSHA_SECRET only (exit 2 if blank), domain-separated HMAC-SHA256, RAM-only linkage/raw state, fail-closed quarantine, PII-free stdout/logs, strict is_relative_to output containment. HMAC is pseudonymization (not encryption), temp cleanup is not secure erasure, and RAM-only execution cannot stop a compromised process—real deployments need host FDE (LUKS/KMS) & vault keys.

Demo Command Center (tools/veil_ui.py — Synthetic Only): Ephemeral random keys when secret omitted, loopback _check_host & strict Origin validation (DNS-rebinding protection), CSP without 'unsafe-eval', X-Frame-Options: DENY, multipart/ZIP streaming bounds, SIGTERM/SIGHUP + dead-PID marker reaping, atomic repair swap, and 0.15s–2.02s UI ready latency up to 50k rows.

7. Disclosed Known Limitations

  • Residual Risk & Unlinked Continuation Rows: Approx. 9–10/48 local re-identification persists under ±180d date-shifted approximate birth years + rare diagnoses/exact LOS. Two-line CSV continuation rows without ID cells (Malayalam H21) link in records.jsonl without altering column structure to inject a PSN- cell.
  • Scope & Verification Boundaries: Unsupported scripts outside Latin + \u0900–\u0D7F abstain cleanly. Passport evidence verifies a specific release pair, not universal anonymity, and hidden evaluation sets remain unseen. Linux container verification approximates but does not reproduce the exact organizer sandbox. Event-wide AI-coding eligibility has not been separately confirmed with organizers.
