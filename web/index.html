Release a hospital dataset without releasing the patients.

VEIL finds personal data across files and scripts, links each patient's records under one keyed pseudonym, generalises the details that could single someone out, and then audits its own output before you share it.

Accepts CSV, TSV, JSON, JSON Lines, FHIR bundles, HL7 v2 and free-text notes in English, Hindi and Hinglish. Up to 64 files and 16 MB per run. Use synthetic data only.

  1. DetectNames, IDs, phones and places in fields, keys, filenames and free text
  2. LinkOne pseudonym per patient across hospitals and scripts; look-alikes kept apart
  3. GeneraliseAges, geography and dates adjusted only where a patient stands out
  4. VerifyAn independent audit checks privacy, clinical values, dates, identity and structure
